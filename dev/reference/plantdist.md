# Dissimilarities of Some U.S. Nuclear Plants

This matrix gives discrepancies between light water nuclear power plants
of two types, seven built on the site of an existing plant and 19 built
on new sites. The discrepancies summarize differences in two covariates
that are predictive of the cost of building a plant.

## Usage

``` r
plantdist
```

## Format

A matrix with 7 rows and 19 columns

## Source

The data appear in Cox, D.R. and Snell, E.J. (1981), *Applied
Statistics: Principles and Examples*, p.82 (Chapman and Hall), and are
due to W.E. Mooz.
